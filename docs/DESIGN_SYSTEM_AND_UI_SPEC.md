The v1.0 **Bazaar Warmth** terracotta palette, the WCAG 2.1 AAA claim, the `h-13` class, and the six-portal layout are retired. The current prototype and the green/orange palette are canonical.

## Retained principles

These principles survive the palette change:

1. **Physical materiality.** Kitchen tickets, printed dockets, receipt-like summaries, and tactile controls.
2. **Glanceability under sunlight.** High contrast, large type, and status labels that do not rely on colour alone.
3. **Thumb-first ergonomics.** Primary actions sit in the bottom reach zone; rider and kitchen targets are oversized.
4. **No generic SaaS styling.** No purple/indigo, pastel blobs, diffuse shadows, stock illustrations, or gratuitous motion.
5. **Bottom sheets on mobile.** Centre-floating dialogs are not used below 768 px.
6. **Tabular numbers.** Prices, timers, PINs, and coordinates never jitter.
