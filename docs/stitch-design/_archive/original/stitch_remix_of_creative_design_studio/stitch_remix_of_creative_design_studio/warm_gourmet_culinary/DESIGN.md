## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an appetizing, high-end yet deeply accessible culinary delivery experience. It blends warm culinary hospitality with modern product design clarity. The visual language conveys freshness, comfort, culinary craft, and effortless convenience.

The core aesthetic builds on **Tactile Warm Modernism**:
- Ultra-soft, warm-tinted off-white foundations evoke linen napkins, artisanal paper menus, and warm kitchen counters rather than sterile digital canvases.
- Saturated warm coral and terracotta accents stimulate appetite, provide clear visual hierarchy, and drive primary conversions.
- Deep, appetizing espresso-charcoal neutrals replace harsh pure blacks, creating high legibility with an organic, premium feel.
- Tactile, deeply rounded cards and pill controls mimic handheld tableware, smooth stones, and physical menu badges.
- Photography takes center stage with high-contrast, overhead or angled presentations of fresh ingredients, surrounded by generous breathing room and warm, diffused ambient shadows.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around appetizing warmth, high contrast, and comforting culinary tones:

- **Primary (`#E8594F`)**: Vibrant terracotta coral. Used for primary call-to-actions, cart badges, active stepper increments, and focal touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#F48C42`)**: Roasted apricot. Used for secondary promotions, tags ("5 Left", "Free delivery"), discount pills, and star ratings.
- **Tertiary (`#4E7B58`)**: Fresh culinary herb green. Reserved for vegetarian badges, health indicators, preparation status ticks, and verified kitchen seals.
- **Neutral (`#2B2220`)**: Rich espresso charcoal. Delivers sharp contrast for typography and primary icons without the coldness of `#000000`.

### Tonal Foundations & Canvas
- **Background Canvas**: `#FBF7F5` (Soft warm buttermilk tint).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards & Sheets)**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure crisp white with warm ambient elevation).
- **Surface Muted / Neutral Low**: `#F3ECE7` (Warm cream wash for inactive steppers, pill track backgrounds, and dividers).
- **Text Secondary / Muted**: `#7E736F` (Warm stone gray for subtext, delivery estimates, and strikethrough original prices).

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across headlines, body copy, and labels. Its friendly geometric curves, generous x-height, and open apertures ensure effortless scanning on compact mobile displays while maintaining a modern, editorial warmth.

- **Headlines**: Set in bold to extra-bold weights (`700`–`800`) with tight negative letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for punchy, friendly authority in greetings (e.g., "Hello, Yahya") and dish titles.
- **Numbers & Pricing**: Rendered in `headline-sm` or `label-lg` with tabular or proportional lining figures to ensure fast visual calculation across list items and checkout summaries.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags**: Set with slight positive tracking (`0.02em`) in `label-sm` for stock warnings, badges ("5 Left"), and distance indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a fluid 4-column structure on mobile, scaling to 8 columns on tablet and 12 columns on desktop web viewports.

- **Horizontal Margins**: Strict `1rem` (16px) to `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewports to maximize usable card surface for food photography, scaling out to `2.5rem` (40px) with centered container caps (`1140px`) on desktop.
- **Gutters**: Set to `0.75rem` (12px) between small grid items (such as square action pills or quick category tiles) and `1rem` (16px) between full dish promo cards.
- **Internal Component Rhythm**:
  - `space-xs` (8px) for micro badge padding and icon-to-label inline gaps.
  - `space-sm` (12px) to `space-md` (16px) for dish card content padding.
  - `space-lg` (24px) for distinct vertical section dividers (e.g., between "Today's Promo" and category filter rows).
- **Floating Overlays**: Safe area dynamic padding at screen bottom accounts for a persistent sticky checkout pill or bottom navigation bar (`space-2xl` clearance).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm-tinted ambient diffusion instead of harsh monochromatic drops. Shadows use the primary espresso neutral (`#2B2220`) at minimal opacities with extended blur radii to mimic soft daylight falling across a table.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#FBF7F5`. Un-elevated background for scrolling feeds.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Stepper Backdrops)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(43, 34, 32, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 34, 32, 0.03)`. Gives menu items and store headers a subtle lift.
- **Level 2 (Active Interactive Cards & Modals)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with `box-shadow: 0 10px 28px -4px rgba(43, 34, 32, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(43, 34, 32, 0.04)`. Applied to active dish inspection sheets, category selection popovers, and checkout sheets.
- **Level 3 (Floating Persistent Controls & CTA Pills)**: Primary colored surfaces or crisp white pills elevated with `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(232, 89, 79, 0.35), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(43, 34, 32, 0.06)`. The warm coral glow anchors the eye on conversion actions like "Checkout" and "Place order".
- **Dividers & Hairlines**: Replaced wherever possible by tonal shifting (`#F3ECE7`) rather than borders, preserving a soft, borderless culinary look.

## Shapes

The shape system is friendly, soft, and tactile, adopting a **Level 3 (Pill-shaped)** baseline:

- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`)**: Applied to all primary CTA buttons, floating checkout bars, quantity steppers, category filter chips, search bars, and status tags.
- **Cards & Sheets (`rounded-3xl` / 24px - 28px)**: Main dish showcase cards, promo hero containers, and bottom navigation sheets use deep, sweeping corner radii to soften viewport edges and harmonize with circular bowl photography.
- **Thumbnails & Media (`rounded-2xl` / 16px - 20px)**: Food image previews, dish thumbnails, and store logos are encased in fluid rounded containers with internal clip paths.
- **Floating Controls**: Round circular buttons (`rounded-full` 40px × 40px) for heart/favorite toggles, back navigation chevrons, and notification bells.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA)**: Full-width or inline pill (`rounded-full`, 48px-52px height). Background `#E8594F`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`. Features an ambient warm glow shadow (`0 10px 24px rgba(232, 89, 79, 0.3)`). Active tap scales down to `0.98`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pill-shaped with `#F3ECE7` background and `#2B2220` text, or transparent with a fine 1.5px `#E8594F` outline.
- **Floating Cart / Checkout Bar**: Persistent bottom floating pill. Displays order summary on the left (item count, price) and directional action on the right ("Checkout" with right arrow). Background `#E8594F`, padded `16px 24px`, sitting 16px above the home indicator.

### Filter & Category Chips
- **Inactive**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `8px 16px`. White surface background, `label-md` `#7E736F` text, accompanied by an 18px colored line icon. Subtle Level 1 elevation.
- **Active / Selected**: Background `#E8594F`, white text and icon, zero border, elevated with Level 2 warm shadow.

### Quantity Stepper (+ / -)
- Compact pill container (`height: 36px`, `rounded-full`, background `#F3ECE7`).
- Decrement (`-`) button: Flat circular target, muted tone `#7E736F`.
- Count indicator: Center-aligned, `label-md`, `#2B2220`.
- Increment (`+`) button: Solid `#E8594F` circular pill, `#FFFFFF` plus glyph.

### Cards
- **Featured Promo Card**: Vertical stack or horizontal split. Pure white background, `rounded-3xl`, padded `12px` to `16px`. Bleed dish image on top or side (`rounded-2xl`). Floating top-right circular favorite button (`#FFFFFF` with `#E8594F` heart). Bottom container displays title, subtitle, price, discount chip, and an inline "+ Add" pill button.
- **Menu List Item**: Horizontal card with an 80px × 80px food thumbnail on the left, centered dish title and delivery time, and the quantity stepper on the far right.

### Input Fields & Search
- Surface `#FFFFFF` or `#F3ECE7`, `height: 48px`, `rounded-full`.
- Left-aligned magnifying icon in `#7E736F`, placeholder text in `body-md` `#7E736F`.
- Focus state activates an outline in `#E8594F` with 2px stroke and a gentle coral outer glow.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Stock / Urgency ("5 Left")**: Miniature pill (`rounded-full`, padding `2px 8px`, `label-sm`), background `#F48C42`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Rating Tag**: Star icon in `#F48C42` paired with `label-sm` bold rating value (e.g., "5.0") in `#2B2220`.
