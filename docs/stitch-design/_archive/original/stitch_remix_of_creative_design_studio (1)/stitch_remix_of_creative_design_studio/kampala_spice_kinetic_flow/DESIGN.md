## Brand & Style

The brand personality captures the unstoppable kinetic velocity of Kampala’s urban rhythm—from vibrant street markets (Nakasero, Owino) and charcoal-fired roasters to the agile, weaving boda-boda logistics grid. It fuses warmth, tactile culinary richness, and relentless operational precision. 

The aesthetic is **Tactile Modernist meets High-Contrast Functionalism**:
- **Culinary Warmth**: Saturated saffron, turmeric, and paprika tones evoke fresh Rolex preparation, grilled street bites, and lush tropical produce.
- **Urban Utility**: High contrast, razor-sharp status delineation, and glare-resistant visual cues engineered for outdoor direct-sunlight viewing on low-cost Android displays, motorcycle handlebars, and greasy kitchen display systems (KDS).
- **Target Audience**: Urban Kampala foodies and office workers seeking dependable D2C meal and grocery replenishment; boda couriers requiring immediate spatial cues; and kitchen expediters managing rapid fire tickets.
- **Emotional Response**: Appetite-stimulating, unmistakably local, dependable, and swift.

## Colors

The palette directly models the physical environment: warm turmeric/paprika saffron anchors consumer appetite; deep slate black delivers structural authority and high-noon contrast; herb green handles verification, success, and delivery handoffs. Functional accents provide native recognition for Ugandan financial flows:

- **Primary (`#D97706` / Paprika Gold)**: Primary actions, cart CTAs, key promotional tags, active order tracking pulses.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` / Charcoal Slate)**: Structural headers, navigation containers, high-emphasis text, bottom navigation bars.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` / Herb Emerald)**: Order delivered, kitchen prep complete, live GPS beacon, affirmative balance updates.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` / Chalk White)**: Base canvas tone, mitigating high-glare ambient reflection outdoors while avoiding eye strain indoors.
- **MTN MoMo Accent (`#FFCC00`)**: Reserved strictly for MTN Mobile Money payment pathways and instant top-ups. Paired with `#000000` text for strict contrast compliance.
- **Airtel Money Accent (`#EF4444` to `#DC2626`)**: Reserved strictly for Airtel Money transactions and balance checks.
- **Warning & Live Prep (`#F97316`)**: Kitchen in-flight baking/grilling status, surge alerts, transit warnings.

## Typography

The type system balances technical punch with humanistic warmth. **Space Grotesk** serves as the display, label, metric, and logistics heading engine—providing clear, open apertures, unmistakable numerics, and immediate tabular distinction for currency (`USh`) and time stamps. **Plus Jakarta Sans** powers all descriptive, narrative, and dense metadata contexts, ensuring maximum reading comfort and rapid scanning on compact screens.

- **Numerics & Currencies**: All pricing amounts must render in `Space Grotesk` with tabular alignment enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). The currency denomination `USh` remains bold and uppercase directly prefixed to the amount with a non-breaking space (e.g., `USh 14,500`).
- **Safety Pin Typography**: The 4-digit handover PIN uses `label-pin` with wide character tracking (`0.25em`) to prevent mistyping or misreading between riders and customers at dusk.
- **Kitchen & Boda Legibility**: In transit or kitchen stations, text drops secondary weights; status counters utilize minimum `18px` bold metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly fluid layout philosophy built to adapt from handheld courier devices (360px width) up to kitchen-mounted Android landscape tablets (1280px width) and dispatch dashboards:

- **Mobile (Base to 599px)**: 4-column fluid grid, `1rem` outer canvas margin, `1rem` gutters. Touch targets must never fall below `48px` vertically to account for one-handed operation or riding gloves.
- **Tablet & Kitchen Displays (600px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1.5rem` outer canvas margins. Adapts KDS tickets into horizontal swimlanes (Received, Prep, Packing, Dispatch).
- **Desktop & Dispatch (1024px+)**: 12-column grid capped at `1280px` max-width container, centered with `2.5rem` margins, enabling concurrent map logistics tracking alongside live vendor queues.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: All layout offsets adhere to a baseline 8px modular scale (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px, `space-2xl` = 48px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Tactile Layering with Warm Ambient Shadowing** alongside **High-Vis Outlines**. Pure black shadows are avoided in favor of charcoal-slate diffusion tinted with warm undertones to prevent muddy UI layers on AMOLED mobile panels:

- **Base Layer (Flat Ground)**: Neutral canvas (`#F8FAFC`), 0px offset.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: White (`#FFFFFF`) surface with a `1px` subtle outline in `#E2E8F0` and an ambient drop shadow: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Action Bars & Delivery Trackers)**: Elevated pill or dock containers hovering above scrollable views: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Bottom Drawers & Urgent KDS Overlays)**: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.20), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Backdrops use a warm scrim: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.
- **Boda Driver & KDS High-Contrast Mode**: In outdoor daylight or steam-heavy kitchen environments, elevation shifts from ambient shadow to a crisp `2px solid #0F172A` high-contrast tactile border.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness factor of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` base corner radius), capturing a balanced, modern, and ergonomic physical feel:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Dropdowns)**: `8px` radius (`rounded-md`).
- **Cards, Kitchen Tickets, Cart Sections**: `16px` radius (`rounded-lg`), creating approachable physical boundaries for menu items and operational modules.
- **Floating Modals, Bottom Sheets, Top Alerts**: `24px` radius on exposed corners (`rounded-xl`).
- **Interactive Badges, MoMo Pills, and Quick Filters**: Full pill radius (`9999px`) for immediate tactile affordance and thumb friendliness.
- **Handover PIN Box**: Distinctive individual square cells (`12px` roundedness) with a thick `2px` inset focus border for digit entry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Checkout, Place Order, Accept Ride)**: Solid `#D97706` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `Space Grotesk` medium-bold typography. Minimum height `48px`. Pressed state shifts to `#B45309`.
- **Secondary (Add to Cart, View Order)**: Deep Slate `#0F172A` background with crisp white text.
- **Tertiary / Outlined**: Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#0F172A`, slate text.
- **MoMo Direct Action**: High-visibility MTN Yellow (`#FFCC00`) with `#000000` bold label, featuring a dedicated icon slot for mobile money iconography.

### Delivery Distance Badges
- **Tier 1 (Free Delivery <= 5km)**: Emerald green tinted pill (`#ECFDF5` background, `#047857` border, `#065F46` text). Label: `TIER 1 • FREE (≤5 KM)`.
- **Tier 2 (Distance Fee <= 15km)**: Saffron yellow tinted pill (`#FFFBEB` background, `#D97706` border, `#B45309` text). Label: `TIER 2 • +USh 1,500/KM`.
- **Out of Range (> 15km)**: Slate pill (`#F1F5F9` background, `#64748B` text).

### Handover 4-Digit PIN Display & Inputs
- **Consumer View**: High-contrast Slate `#0F172A` box containing large spaced numbers in `Space Grotesk` (`label-pin`). Accompanied by the caption: *“Share only when boda driver arrives with food”*.
- **Rider Verification Input**: Four discrete `56px x 64px` boxes with numeric-only focus keys, validating instantaneously with green pulse animations upon correctness.

### Kitchen Display System (KDS) Status Indicators
- **New Order**: Pulsing amber badge (`#FEF3C7` background, `#D97706` text) with a live minute counter (`+3m`).
- **In Cooking / Grill**: High-heat orange badge (`#FFEDD5` background, `#EA580C` text).
- **Ready for Boda Pickup**: Emerald green banner (`#10B981` background, `#FFFFFF` text) triggering high-volume dispatch.

### Currency (UGX / USh) Price Displays
- Always formatted with explicit spacing: `USh 28,000`.
- Strikethrough promotional pricing keeps original price in `#94A3B8` slate with discount in `#10B981`.

### Cards & Menu Tiles
- **Food Card**: Image with a 4:3 aspect ratio, subtle inner gradient to protect bottom text legibility, dynamic distance badge docked at top right, and bold price tag anchored at bottom left.
- **Sticky Courier Bottom Sheet**: Houses active live-driver status, boda bike license plate pill, one-tap direct call button, and the handover security code.

### Input Fields
- Minimum `48px` height with `1.5px` border in `#CBD5E1`. Focused state transitions to `#D97706` border with a subtle 3px gold aura (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`). Helper text in `12px` Plus Jakarta Sans.
