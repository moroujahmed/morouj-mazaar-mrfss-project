Colour & contrast

Buttons & actions

One primary action per view. Orange means “do this next”; green means navigation or confirmation; red only appears for destructive work.

Variants

Sizes & icon buttons

Customer targets are at least 48px. Rider actions are 56–66px so they remain reachable with one hand and gloves.

Choice & switch

Feedback

System messages say what happened, what it means and what to do next. Critical messages use red plus an alert icon and direct wording.

Payment confirmedYour order is with the kitchen. We will update the tracker as each step is completed.
Weak connectionKeep this screen open until the delivery confirmation appears.
Handover could not be verifiedAsk the customer to read the 4-digit PIN again, or call dispatch.
Free delivery within 5 kmNakasero, Kololo and Nakawa are inside the free zone.

Progress

Toasts are announced politely; error toasts use role="alert". Nothing disappears before a person can read it.

Surfaces & tokens

Cards use a single-pixel border and a low shadow. Elevation increases only when a surface becomes interactive or floats above the page.

Accessibility rules

These are build requirements, not suggestions. A page that breaks one of them is not ready to ship.

ContrastBody and UI text meet WCAG 2.2 AA. White is never placed on orange.
FocusA 3px green focus ring is always visible; dark surfaces switch to orange.
Targets48px minimum on customer and admin screens; 56px minimum on rider screens.
Never colour-onlyStatus, errors and connection state always include an icon and text.
Reduced motionAnimations collapse to near-zero when the operating system asks for less motion.
